<h1>DistroKid Billing-State Workflow Report</h1>

<p>This repository documents a potential billing-state or catalog-availability issue observed in DistroKid.</p>

<h2>Purpose</h2>

<p>The purpose of this report is to document a possible platform flaw in a responsible way.</p>

<p>This is <strong>not</strong> a guide for bypassing payment, reactivating content without authorization, avoiding normal platform fees, or reproducing the issue publicly.</p>

<h2>Summary</h2>

<p>While reviewing my own account and publicly visible music listings, I observed that certain releases appeared to remain available on third-party music platforms even though my visible account state did not appear to support that result.</p>

<p>The concern is that billing-state, catalog-state, or release-availability checks may not be fully aligned across account status, release management, and store availability.</p>

<h2>Scope</h2>

<p>This public write-up intentionally omits reproduction steps, private URLs, release identifiers, invoice data, screenshots, and account-specific details.</p>
